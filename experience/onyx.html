ONYX Cinema technology

ONYX Cinema technology, developed by Samsung, is a high-end LED cinema display technology designed to deliver high brightness (with a peak brightness of up to 146 foot-lamberts) and near-infinite contrast. These two features allow the screen to produce the purest whites and deepest blacks, enhancing the color contrast and creating a smooth, seamless visual experience. It is especially suited for high dynamic range (HDR) content and 3D movie screenings, providing an advanced and immersive visual feast.






ONYX Cinema is also equipped with JBL by HARMAN's professional audio system, providing an immersive sound experience for the audience. By integrating speakers along the edge of the screen, ONYX technology creates an expanded audio "sweet spot" throughout the theater, ensuring that all seats experience clear and powerful sound, further enhancing the viewing experience. This allows the audience to not only enjoy sharp visual effects but also experience deeper, more dynamic audio.
