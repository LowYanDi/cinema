DLP Cinema technology

A DLP screen is a type of projection technology based on Digital Light Processing, widely used in many cinemas. This technology utilizes a Digital Micromirror Device (DMD) and tiny mirrors to create images. The screen typically uses a projector to display the image on a large screen, resulting in high-quality visuals. Due to the DMD chip's ability to rapidly adjust the angle of the mirrors, DLP projectors deliver exceptionally smooth motion, making them ideal for displaying fast-moving images. In LTD cinemas, audiences can even enjoy DLP screens with up to 4K resolution, providing an immersive experience, especially when watching action films.






In LTD cinemas, audiences can enjoy DLP screens as large as 100 square meters, which provide sufficient visual impact without losing detail.
