Synopsis

“The haunted, calling voices will be utterly terrifying.” Three years after the death of Yam, Yak still relentlessly hunts the Black Spirit that took his sister’s life. Despite his family's efforts to convince him to stop seeking revenge on the Black Spirit, Yak remains stubborn, determined to track it down, fearing that the ghost will return to harm the remaining members of his family. Yak follows the mysterious death of a newborn, discovered in a newspaper article, and finds clues leading him to the fact that the Black Spirit is being controlled by a powerful shaman named 'Puang,'' who fled to hide in the 'Dong Khomot,'' a place filled with mystical and dangerous powers. Yak must confront malevolent spirits and dangers around him in order to defeat the Black Spirit, ensuring it never returns to harm his family again. Meanwhile, Yad is about to marry 'Pradit,'' and the family is preparing for their wedding, hoping that Yak will return to attend the ceremony and reunite with everyone. Little do they know, the Black Spirit is about to return, bringing terror to Yak's family once again.
