About us
                                                           
Lim Tee Di(LTD) Cinema is a world-renowned chain of brother enterprises that was created by Lim Zhun Zi, Tee Jun Hao, and Low Yan Di and ranks among the top 5 cinemas globally. Known for its innovative, advanced cinematic technology, events, and exceptional customer service. Operating in over 20 countries, such as Austria, LTD Cinema has a strong global presence. It has a total of 1987 screens across the world and the most operating locations in Malaysia, which have 189 screens. We have the largest cinema, located in Tunku Abdul Rahman University of Management and Technology (TARUMT), Malaysia. LTD Cinema has set a new standard in the movie going experience. LTD Cinema has also invested heavily in the latest projection and sound systems. It allows customers to experience an unprecedented immersive experience, making it the first choice in the minds of the public.
